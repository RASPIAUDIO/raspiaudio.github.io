CoreZero S3 – Digital Radio

FM and DAB+ radio on a ZeroCore S3 fitted with the RASPIAUDIO Digital Radio 40-pin shield (SI4689). Control the shield from a local web page or USB serial. Audio plays from the shield's analog jack or speaker amplifier; it is not streamed to the browser.

This factory installation replaces the ESP32 firmware and settings. It overwrites the existing ZeroCore application. Choose to erase the device for a clean installation. The download contains no home Wi-Fi credentials.

  1. Power off, fit the Digital Radio shield to the ZeroCore S3 40-pin header, and connect the external antenna.
  2. Connect a USB data cable to the ZeroCore S3 USB-C port. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, then click Connect below.
  3. After installation, join the RASPIAUDIO-Radio-XXXXXX hotspot (password raspiaudio) and open http://192.168.4.1/.
  4. To use your own 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, enter its SSID and password in the page's Connexion section. If connection fails, the hotspot returns automatically.

Use the page to tune FM or DAB+, scan a band, choose a DAB service, change the analog volume, and control the shield amplifier. The serial commands remain available at 115200 baud.
