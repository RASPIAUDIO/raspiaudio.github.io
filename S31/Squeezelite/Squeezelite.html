S31 - Squeezelite (experimental)

Ethernet and Wi-Fi audio player with Squeezelite, AirPlay 1, Bluetooth Classic and Sendspin. Spotify Connect playback remains under investigation.

New feature to test: configure Wi-Fi over USB with Improv Serial immediately after flashing. No BLE setup is required.

Uses a dedicated S31-compatible installer. Existing settings are preserved. Fresh installations use the onboard ES8311 audio output; external DACs and displays need their own settings.
